cartly

One product, every store. Cartly compares grocery prices across Toronto retailers so you always know where your basket costs less.

What Cartly does

  • Best price: the lowest price for the exact same product, across stores.
  • Best value: the lowest price per unit, so bigger packs are compared fairly.
  • Cheaper alternatives: similar products from other brands, clearly labelled.
  • One list: build a shopping list from the cheapest offers and open each item at its store.

Stores compared

Walmart, Real Canadian Superstore, Loblaws, Metro, No Frills, FreshCo, Shoppers Drug Mart, Rexall, Costco and Dollarama, with prices collected across Toronto.
