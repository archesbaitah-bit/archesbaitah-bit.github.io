Cartly Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

Cartly is a grocery price-comparison app for Toronto, Canada. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and, if you provide it, your name. Cartly does not collect phone numbers. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from that provider.
  • Delivery addresses. Addresses you choose to save in the app (label, street, city, postal code and delivery notes) are stored with your account so they are ready when ordering features become available.
  • Shopping list. Your shopping list is stored on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers.

We do not collect payment card information, and the app does not track your precise location.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To store and show your saved delivery addresses.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and sharing

Account information and saved addresses are stored with our backend provider, Supabase, in Canada, and are transmitted over encrypted connections. Information is shared only with the sign-in provider you choose (Google or Apple) to authenticate you, and as required by law.

Retention and deletion

Your account information is kept while your account is active. You may ask us to delete your account and associated personal information at any time by emailing arch.esbaitah@gmail.com; we will complete deletion within 30 days.

Children

Cartly is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
