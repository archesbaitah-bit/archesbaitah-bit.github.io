Cartly Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Cartly ("Cartly", "we", "us") is a grocery price comparison service for Toronto, currently in testing. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: otp.cartly.app@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create a Cartly account we collect your email address and a password (stored in hashed form by our authentication provider). You may also sign in with Google or Apple, in which case we receive your email address and name from that provider.
  • Profile and addresses. If you save a profile or delivery addresses in the app, we store what you enter so the app can use it for your orders and comparisons.
  • App activity. Searches, viewed products, and shopping lists you create, so those features work across your sessions.
  • What we do not collect. We do not collect phone numbers. We do not take or store payment card details in the app at this time.

Price data

Product prices shown in Cartly are recorded from publicly listed retailer storefronts on grocery platforms. Each price carries the date it was recorded. Prices are observations, not offers: they may differ in store or at checkout, and Cartly is not affiliated with the retailers or platforms named.

How we use information

  • To operate your account and provide the comparison, list, and ordering features.
  • To send account emails (sign-in codes, confirmations) and, with your consent, product updates.
  • To publish Cartly's own social content (Instagram @thecartlyapp, TikTok @cartly.app) generated from recorded price data. Some Cartly presenters in videos are AI-generated and are disclosed as such.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database and authentication), GitHub Pages (this website), Google (email delivery and optional sign-in), Apple (optional sign-in), Meta/Instagram and TikTok (publishing Cartly's own content). These providers process information only as needed to provide their services.

Retention and your choices

Account information is kept while your account exists. You may ask us at any time to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information by emailing otp.cartly.app@gmail.com; we will respond within 30 days. Deleting your account removes your profile, addresses, and lists.

Children

Cartly is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and note the new effective date.
