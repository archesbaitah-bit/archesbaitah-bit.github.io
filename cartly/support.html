Grocery price comparison for Toronto

Cartly compares grocery prices for the same product across Toronto stores and platforms, so you can see where an item costs less before you shop. Need help? Email us at otp.cartly.app@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

Common questions

  • Where do the prices come from? Prices are recorded from publicly listed retailer storefronts on grocery platforms. Every price in the app shows the store, the platform, and the date it was recorded.
  • Why does a price differ in store? Prices are observations, not offers. Retailers change prices at any time, so the price you see in store or at checkout can differ from the recorded price shown in Cartly.
  • Can I order groceries through Cartly? Not yet. Cartly is a comparison tool: tapping a price takes you to the store on the platform where that price was recorded.
  • A price or product looks wrong. What can I do? Open the product in the app and use the report option on the product page. Reports are reviewed and corrected data is updated in the app.
  • How do I sign in? You can create an account with your email address, or sign in with Google or Apple. Sign-in codes are sent to your email.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Profile and choose Delete account. Your account and your saved data are permanently removed.
